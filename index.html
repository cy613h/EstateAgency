    Our Services

    Buy, sell or rent in Nairobi with people who pick up the phone.

    Property sales

    We price your home realistically, photograph it properly, market it to serious buyers and manage every viewing and offer until the sale closes.

    Lettings

    We find and vet tenants, prepare tenancy agreements and handle deposits, so your property earns steadily and stays in good hands.

    Property management

    Rent collection, repairs, inspections and monthly statements, all handled for you. One call to us replaces the calls from tenants.

    Valuations

    A free, honest valuation based on recent sales and rents in your area, so you know what your property is really worth before you decide.

    Buyer search

    Tell us your budget, area and must-haves. We shortlist matching homes, arrange viewings and check the title before you commit.

    Advice and paperwork

    Straight answers on offers, negotiation, sale agreements and what to expect at each stage, with introductions to trusted lawyers and valuers.
